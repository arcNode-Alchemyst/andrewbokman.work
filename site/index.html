02Profile

I’ve run production in a custom meat shop, on a multi-product cereal line, and now across a cheese and whey plant.

On the floor

I work as a Resource Operator II at Agropur, a cross-trained operator in a cheese and whey plant on 12-hour nights. Before that I spent five years at Post Consumer Brands, promoted from Processing Operator to Processing Specialist.

My experience covers startups, changeovers and shutdowns, process monitoring and troubleshooting, CIP and sanitation, sampling and testing, lockout/tagout and confined-space work, SAP, and the Wonderware HMI.

Leading and training

At Post I directed 4–5 operators as Processing Specialist and trained operators on equipment, process and troubleshooting. At Herda’s I opened the shop, directed 2–3 employees and ran the operation when the owner was away.

At Agropur I train operators and cover for the supervisor when needed.

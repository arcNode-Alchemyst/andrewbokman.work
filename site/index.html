02Profile

The foundation

I currently work as a Resource Operator II with Agropur. My broader manufacturing background includes production, processing, operations supervision, operator training, troubleshooting, process control, startups and changeovers, quality systems, and reliability-focused work.

My experience includes continuous improvement, OEE and downtime reduction, root cause analysis, GMP/HACCP environments, equipment troubleshooting, operator training, SOP development, SAP/Oracle, MES systems, industrial HMIs, and Allen-Bradley and Siemens-based environments.

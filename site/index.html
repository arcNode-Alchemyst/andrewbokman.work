02Profile

Process and production operator with 15+ years in food manufacturing: hands-on equipment operation, troubleshooting, operator training and supervisory coverage on 12-hour shifts.

The foundation

I currently work as a Resource Operator II with Agropur, a cross-trained coverage operator in a cheese and whey plant on 12-hour nights. My broader manufacturing background includes production, processing, running day-to-day operations, operator training, troubleshooting, process control, startups and changeovers, sanitation and testing, quality systems, and reliability-focused work.

My experience includes continuous improvement, OEE and downtime reduction, root cause analysis, GMP/HACCP environments, equipment troubleshooting, operator training, SOP development, SAP, and industrial HMIs including Wonderware.

The layer being added

Alongside my manufacturing career, I am completing a B.S. in Computer Science at Rasmussen University and continuing to develop skills in Python, Linux, Git/GitHub, Docker, networking, automation, and software systems.

My long-term direction is at the intersection of operations and technology: industrial automation, technical operations, intelligent manufacturing systems, software, and AI-enabled process improvement.
